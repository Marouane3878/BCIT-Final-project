Meet the coaching team behind Spartan Fight Club. Our coaches bring experience, discipline, and a strong commitment to helping every student improve with confidence. Whether your goal is fitness, self-defence, technical development, or competition, our team is here to guide you with professional instruction and support.

Our Coaches

At Spartan Fight Club, coaching is built on skill, discipline, safety, and respect. Each coach focuses on helping students develop strong fundamentals, train with purpose, and progress at a level that matches their goals and experience.

Coach Daniel Reyes

Coach Daniel leads our striking program with a strong focus on fundamentals, movement, timing, and technical precision. His classes help students build confidence while developing solid offensive and defensive habits through structured drills, pad work, and controlled live training.

Coaching Focus

  • Stance, balance, and footwork
  • Punching and kicking combinations
  • Defensive reactions and timing
  • Conditioning and fight discipline

Coach Marcus Silva

Coach Marcus teaches Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu with an emphasis on clean technique, control, and smart decision-making. He works closely with beginners and advanced students alike, helping them understand positioning, transitions, and submissions in a clear and professional way.

Coaching Focus

  • Positional control and escapes
  • Submissions and submission defence
  • Grip work and transitions
  • Technical awareness under pressure

Coach Aiden Brooks

Coach Aiden leads the wrestling program and focuses on takedowns, pressure, chain wrestling, and athletic movement. His coaching style pushes students to become more explosive, more disciplined, and more confident in stand-up grappling and overall body control.

Coaching Focus

  • Stance and level changes
  • Takedowns and clinch entries
  • Balance, pressure, and pace control
  • Strength, conditioning, and mental toughness

What Our Coaches Value

Our coaching team shares the same standards across every class. Students are encouraged to train with discipline, consistency, and respect while focusing on long-term progress instead of short-term results.

Technical Development

Coaches place strong emphasis on fundamentals, repetition, and clean execution. Students are taught how to build skills correctly before increasing intensity.

Safe Training

Safety is a priority in every class. Coaches make sure students train with control, proper equipment, and awareness of their partners and surroundings.

Student Progress

Every student progresses differently. Our coaches help members move forward at a steady and realistic pace based on their goals, experience, and commitment.

Why Train With Our Coaching Team

A strong gym is built on strong leadership. Our coaches are committed to creating a professional training environment where students can improve physically and mentally while feeling supported at every stage.

Clear Instruction

  • Simple and direct teaching methods
  • Step-by-step technical breakdowns
  • Coaching for all experience levels

Strong Discipline

  • Professional standards in every class
  • Focus on consistency and effort
  • Respect for teammates and coaches

Supportive Environment

  • Welcoming atmosphere for beginners
  • Guidance for students with different goals
  • Encouragement without losing structure

Goal-Based Training

  • Fitness and conditioning support
  • Technical growth for serious students
  • Preparation for sparring and competition

Coach Expectations

To get the most out of every session, students are expected to follow coach instructions, arrive prepared, and contribute to a respectful and focused gym culture.

  • Be on time: Arrive ready to train and respect class start times.
  • Listen carefully: Follow technical and safety instructions throughout the session.
  • Train with control: Good training partners help each other improve safely.
  • Stay consistent: Progress comes from discipline and regular effort.
  • Show respect: Respect coaches, teammates, and the training space at all times.
  • Ask questions: Coaches are here to help students understand and improve.
