Programs

Find ourstructured martial arts programs designed to help you find the training style that suits you best. Whether you are new to training or looking to refine your skills, you can explore each discipline and make an informed decision based on your goals. Our programs are built for youth and adults, men and women, and all experience levels. Whether your goal is to compete, improve your fitness, build confidence, or lose weight, there is a path here for you.

Striking

Our striking program focuses on clean technique, movement, timing, and power. Students develop strong fundamentals through pad work, partner drills, bag work, and conditioning.

What You Will Work On

  • Stance, footwork, and balance
  • Punches, kicks, and combinations
  • Defence, timing, and distance management
  • Cardio, endurance, and sharp technique under pressure

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Our Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu program focuses on control, technique, problem solving, and self-defence. Students learn how to manage positions, apply submissions, escape pressure, and build confidence through technical grappling.

What You Will Work On

  • Positional control and transitions
  • Escapes, sweeps, and reversals
  • Submissions and submission defence
  • Patience, strategy, and live grappling awareness

Wrestling

Our wrestling program develops strong takedowns, balance, pressure, and explosivemovement. Students improve stand-up control, athletic conditioning, and the ability to dictate pace and position during training.

What You Will Work On

  • Stance, level changes, and movement
  • Takedowns and clinch control
  • Balance, pressure, and chain wrestling
  • Conditioning, discipline, and mental toughness

Skill Levels

Our programs are structured to support students at every stage of training. Whether you are stepping onto the mats for the first time or preparing for advanced sparring and competition, each level is designed to match your current ability while helping you progress safely and confidently.

Beginner

The beginner level is designed for students who are new to martial arts or returning after a long break. Classes focus on building a strong foundation through basic stance, movement, balance, coordination, and essential techniques. Students also learn gym etiquette, partner respect, and how to train with control.

This level is ideal for anyone who wants to improve fitness, build confidence, learn self-defence, or become comfortable in a structured training environment before moving into more advanced work.

Intermediate

The intermediate level is for students who already understand the fundamentals and are ready to improve timing, combinations, control, transitions, and overall training awareness. Classes become more detailed and demanding, with greater focus on applying technique in live drills and controlled partner work.

This level is well suited for students who want to sharpen their skills, increase training intensity, and develop a more complete understanding of their discipline while continuing to build consistency and discipline.

Advanced

The advanced level is intended for experienced students who have a strong technical base and are ready for a faster pace, higher intensity, and more complex strategy. Training at this level focuses on precision, problem solving, adaptability, conditioning, and performance under pressure.

This level is ideal for committed students and competitors who want to push their ability further, refine advanced techniques, and prepare for higher-level sparring, tournaments, or long-term athletic development.

Gear Checklist

Bringing the right equipment helps you train safely, stay comfortable, and get the most out of each session. Students should always arrive prepared with clean training clothes, personal hygiene items, and the required gear for their discipline.

General Training Essentials

  • Water bottle to stay hydrated throughout class
  • Clean athletic clothing that allows free movement
  • Towel and personal hygiene items
  • Flip-flops or slides for walking outside the mat area
  • A small bag to keep all training gear organized

Striking Gear

  • Boxing gloves suitable for training and pad work
  • Hand wraps for wrist and knuckle protection
  • Mouthguard for contact drills and sparring
  • Shin guards when required for kick-based classes
  • Athletic shorts and shirt that allow fast movement

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Gear

  • Gi and belt for gi classes
  • Rash guard and grappling shorts for no-gi training
  • Water bottle and towel
  • Trimmed nails to protect training partners
  • Clean clothing and gear before every class

Wrestling Gear

  • Athletic shirt and shorts for unrestricted movement
  • Wrestling shoes if required by the class or coach
  • Knee pads if extra support is needed
  • Mouthguard for partner drills when appropriate
  • Water bottle and a change of clothes after training

Safety Rules

Safety is a core part of training. Every student is expected to help create a respectful, disciplined, and controlled environment where everyone can learn and improve with confidence.

  • Listen to coaches at all times: Instructions are given to keep training organized, technical, and safe for everyone in class.
  • Train with control: Good technique and partner safety always come before power or ego, especially during live drills and sparring.
  • Respect your training partners: Be professional, cooperative, and aware of your partner's experience level and physical limits.
  • Keep gear and clothing clean: Hygiene is essential in all martial arts training and helps maintain a healthy gym environment.
  • Remove jewelry and trim nails: This reduces the risk of scratches, cuts, and unnecessary injuries during practice.
  • Tap early when needed: In grappling classes, tapping is a normal and important part of safe training and should never be ignored.
  • Report injuries immediately: If you feel pain, dizziness, or discomfort, inform a coach right away and stop training if necessary.
  • Stay focused during class: Pay attention, follow directions, and avoid horseplay so the training space remains safe and professional.
