Schedule Notes and Policies

* Gi Required for Gi Classes

Students attending any BJJ class marked with an asterisk (*) must wear a gi. A clean gi and belt are required for participation. Students without the proper uniform may be asked to join a no-gi session instead, if available.

** Sparring Classes

Sparring sessions require the correct safety equipment. Students must bring a mouthguard for all sparring classes. For striking sparring, gloves, shin guards, and other required protective gear must be worn. Students are expected to train with control and follow all coach instructions.

Arrival and Punctuality

Please arrive at least 10 to 15 minutes before class begins. This allows enough time to change, prepare equipment, and be ready for warm-up. Late arrivals may be asked to wait until a safe point in class before joining.

Hygiene and Clean Gear

All students are expected to maintain good hygiene and wear clean training clothes and equipment. Nails should be trimmed, and all gear should be cleaned regularly. This is especially important for grappling classes and partner drills.

Skill Levels

Beginner classes are designed for newer students learning fundamentals. Intermediate and advanced classes are for students with more training experience. If you are not sure which class is right for you, speak with a coach before joining.

Class Changes

Class times and instructors may occasionally change because of holidays, events, or gym programming. Please check with the gym or contact us directly for the most up-to-date schedule.

Need Help Choosing a Class?

Contact us and we will help you find the right class based on your experience, goals, and training interests.
